📻 FM Radio Receiver Using BF494 and LM386

Overview

This project is a simple FM Radio Receiver built using BF494 transistors and an LM386 audio amplifier IC. The receiver uses an LC tuning circuit to capture FM broadcast signals and converts them into audible sound through a speaker.

The project was designed and assembled on a perfboard as a practical demonstration of radio frequency (RF) communication, signal tuning, demodulation, and audio amplification.



Features

- Receives FM radio broadcasts (88–108 MHz)
- Adjustable tuning using a 22 pF trimmer capacitor
- Audio amplification using LM386
- Compact and portable design
- Battery-powered operation (4.5V)
- Built using easily available electronic components



Components Used

- 2 × BF494 Transistors
- 1 × LM386 Audio Amplifier IC
- 1 × 22 pF Trimmer Capacitor
- 1 × 22 kΩ Resistor
- 1 × 10 kΩ Preset Potentiometer
- 1 × 0.22 µF Capacitor
- 1 × 0.1 µF Capacitor
- 1 × 220 µF Electrolytic Capacitor
- Air-core Coil (4 turns, 18 SWG wire)
- Telescopic Antenna
- Speaker / Headphone
- 4.5V Battery
- Perfboard


Working Principle

The telescopic antenna receives FM radio signals from nearby broadcasting stations. The LC tank circuit, formed by the air-core coil and trimmer capacitor, selects a specific FM frequency. The BF494 transistor stages process and demodulate the received signal. The recovered audio signal is then amplified by the LM386 audio amplifier and played through the speaker.



Testing Results

The receiver was successfully tested with a telescopic antenna and battery supply.

Results:

- Successfully received local FM radio stations.
- Tuned to Bangladesh Betar broadcasts.
- Clear audio output was obtained through the speaker.
- Music and radio programs were heard successfully.
- Stable operation achieved through manual tuning of the trimmer capacitor.
